# Hyper-Cut AI: Minimal Video Editor & Hyperframes Engine

A minimal, agentic video editor featuring conventional multi-track non-linear editing (NLE), real-time canvas preview, prompt-to-edit AI actions, spatial canvas circling/highlighting, and HeyGen **Hyperframes** deterministic graphics.

---

## ✨ Features

1. **Spatial Canvas Circling & Area Highlighting**:
   - Toggle **"Circle Canvas Area"** on the player bar.
   - Click and drag directly on the video preview canvas to circle or highlight any region (face, logo, object, background).
   - Instant floating contextual action badge appears:
     - **👉 Arrow Here**: Generates a bouncing neon vector arrow pointing directly at the circled coordinate.
     - **🔒 Blur Region**: Applies a hardware-accelerated privacy/censor backdrop blur mask over the exact bounding box.
     - **🎯 Spotlight Area**: Creates an animated glowing radar callout focusing viewer attention on the area.
   - The AI Prompting Panel automatically detects the active region and customizes prompts to target those coordinates.

2. **Conventional Multi-Track Timeline (NLE)**:
   - **Track 1 (V1)**: Main Video Footage
   - **Track 2 (V2)**: Hyperframes Deterministic Overlays & Motion Graphics
   - **Track 3 (C1)**: AI Subtitles & Kinetic Captions
   - **Track 4 (A1)**: Audio & Background Music with Auto-Ducking
   - Draggable playhead, zoom controls, left/right trim handles, and clip re-ordering.

3. **AI Prompting Panel (Natural Language Video Directing)**:
   - Plain-English video editing commands:
     - `Cut silence between 3 and 7` → Detects time boundaries, ripples cuts, and closes gaps.
     - `Add kinetic title "THE FUTURE OF AI" with Hyperframes` → Compiles vector HTML/CSS/GSAP and places it on the overlay track.
     - `Add lower third "Alex Rivera" "AI Systems Lead"` → Generates frosted-glass presenter badge.
     - `Auto-generate captions` → Synthesizes word-synced animated subtitles.
     - `Add animated subscribe and bell CTA badge` → Inserts pulsing YouTube/Social CTA.
     - `Add background music with 25% vocal ducking` → Automatically levels audio.
     - `Split clip at playhead` → Slices clip at current playhead position.

4. **Hyperframes Studio & Code Inspector**:
   - Inspect and live-edit the raw HTML/CSS/GSAP code generated by the AI for any animated clip.
   - Real-time sandbox preview with frame-accurate timing.

5. **Multi-Format Preview & Export**:
   - Toggle between **16:9 Landscape** (YouTube, Desktop) and **9:16 Portrait** (TikTok, Shorts, Reels).
   - In-browser client-side video export.
   - Copyable hardware-accelerated **FFmpeg CLI command** for local lossless exports.

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Space` | Play / Pause playback |
| `S` | Split selected clip at playhead |
| `Delete` / `Backspace` | Delete selected clip |
| `Escape` | Clear active canvas annotation |
| `←` / `→` (Arrows) | Seek backward / forward 1s |
